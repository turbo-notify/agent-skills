# The business

Interview guide. Ask, write in the person's own words and store with `kb-write` (public).

Questions:
- What do customers call the business? What does it do, in a sentence or two?
- What days and hours does it answer? Different holiday hours?
- Address, landmark, online only or also a shop.
- Phone, e-mail or website a customer may use (only what may be said).

Final file (shape example):
Name and what it does.
Hours: days and times.
Address or way of serving.
Contact that may be shared.
